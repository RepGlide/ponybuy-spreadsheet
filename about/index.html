About This Resource

A Shortlist Editing method and magnetic cut board built around a shared PonyBuy spreadsheet.

What this resource is

This is an independent informational and navigation resource for people who use a PonyBuy spreadsheet. It offers a Shortlist Editing method — Keep, Hold, and Cut — so each candidate keeps a clear reason and the list stays focused.

What this resource is not

This resource is not the official PonyBuy website, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by PonyBuy or Google. It is not a recommendation list, a product database, a review platform, a store, a QC certification tool, a shopping agent, an order tracker, or a fee calculator.

The Shortlist Editing method

Everything here follows three states: Keep (relevant and worth checking), Hold (missing context, no conclusion yet), and Cut (out of scope, duplicate, or no longer matching the page). An empty slot is allowed. Being curated, best, verified, trusted, or safe never equals a verified product or a reason to buy.

How to use it

  1. Open the shared Google Sheet to see the listing references.
  2. Browse the twelve product categories from the home page.
  3. Read the three guides before building a shortlist.
  4. Keep, hold, or cut each entry with a reason.

Editing example — not a recommendation, ranking, verified list, or saved shortlist.
