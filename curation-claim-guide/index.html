Curation Claim Guide

See what curated, best, verified, trusted, and safe require as evidence, and why inclusion is not verification.

Inclusion is a decision, not a proof

Words like curated, best, verified, trusted, and safe describe a selection someone made. A selection is a claim, not evidence. Read these labels for what they are and what they still require.

Curated and included

Being in a list or a spreadsheet means someone chose to include it. It does not say who chose it, on what basis, or whether the choice holds for you.

Best and trusted

"Best" is a ranking claim and "trusted" is an impression. Neither is a measurement you can check against a source page on its own.

Verified and safe

"Verified" and "safe" imply a check took place. Unless the check and its basis are visible, the label is still just a claim.

What a label would need

  1. A source — who made the claim and on what basis.
  2. A scope — what exactly was checked or selected.
  3. A current page — the claim re-read against the page you open now.
Edit noteInclusion is not verification. A label tells you it was picked; it does not tell you it is right for you.

A worked reading

  1. Label: the row says "curated".
  2. Source: not shown.
  3. Scope: not stated.
  4. Current page: still to be re-read.
  5. Result: treated as a claim, not a verification.

Editing example — not a recommendation, ranking, verified list, or saved shortlist.
