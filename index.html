Curated Is a Selection Claim, Not Proof

Inclusion Is Not Verification

Words like curated, best, verified, trusted, and safe describe a selection, not a guarantee.

✓What a curation claim says

  • Someone included or selected this item.
  • It is labeled curated, best, verified, trusted, or safe.
  • A selection decision made by someone else.

✕What it cannot prove

  • That the product has actually been verified.
  • That it is worth buying for your purpose.
  • That it is authentic, quality, or currently available.
Edit noteA label tells you it was picked. It does not tell you why, or whether the pick holds for you.

Cut Without Filling the Gap

Leave the Empty Slot Empty

  • 1

    Link has changed. The destination no longer matches the row. Stop and cut or re-verify.

  • 2

    Context is missing. A key detail is absent. Stop and hold until it is filled.

  • 3

    Role is duplicated. Two rows play the same role. Stop and keep one, cut the other.

  • 4

    Conditions fail. Key conditions no longer match. Stop and cut — do not guess a replacement to fill the gap.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a PonyBuy spreadsheet?

A PonyBuy spreadsheet is a shared document that compiles listing references, category groupings, and product links formatted for use with agent shopping services. It is a reference to research, not an official, live, or complete product catalog.

Is this website the official PonyBuy service?

No. This website is an independent informational and navigation resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by PonyBuy or Google.

What do Keep, Hold, and Cut mean?

Keep means the candidate is relevant and worth checking further. Hold means key context is missing, so it stays for now without a conclusion. Cut means the candidate is out of scope, a duplicate, or no longer matches the current destination page. An empty slot is also allowed — the list can stay unfilled.

Does curated mean verified?

No. Being included, curated, best, verified, trusted, or safe does not equal a verified product or a reason to buy. These are selection claims, not proof of authenticity, quality, or current availability.

Does this site recommend or rank products?

No. This resource is not a recommendation list, a product database, a review platform, a store, a QC certification tool, a shopping agent, an order tracker, or a fee calculator. It does not rank products or make automatic recommendations.

When should I remove something from my shortlist?

Hold or cut a candidate when the link has changed, key context is missing, its role is duplicated, or key conditions no longer match. Cut without filling the gap — an empty slot is better than a guessed entry.
