Remove from Shortlist Guide

Hold or cut an entry when a link changes, context is missing, a role repeats, or key conditions fail.

Removing is editing, not failure

A shortlist stays useful because you remove what no longer belongs. Hold what is temporarily unclear and cut what is out of scope, so the list keeps earning its length.

Hold — the link has changed

If the destination no longer matches the row, hold the entry and re-verify before acting on it. Do not cut it on a guess.

Hold — context is missing

If a key detail is absent, hold the entry until the context is filled. A missing detail is a pause, not a removal.

Cut — role is duplicated

If two rows play the same role, keep one and cut the other. A duplicate does not make the shortlist stronger.

Cut — key conditions fail

If the category, use, or necessary options no longer match, cut the entry. Do not guess a replacement to fill the gap.

Edit noteHold keeps a question open; Cut closes an out-of-scope entry. Neither needs a made-up answer to fill the slot.
!Open item: has an entry been kept only because cutting it would leave the list looking empty?

A worked removal

  1. Link changed: the page no longer matches — held for re-verification.
  2. Context missing: material unstated — held.
  3. Role duplicated: two near-identical rows — one kept, one cut.
  4. Conditions failed: wrong size — cut.
  5. Result: the list is shorter and still honest.

Editing example — not a recommendation, ranking, verified list, or saved shortlist.
